All models use a common weighted suitability framework: local need/demand 25%, infrastructure readiness 20%, economic value 20%, environmental benefit 20%, implementation feasibility 15%. Technical factors above inform the evidence entered for each dimension. A missing or non-applicable resource should be marked N/A rather than scored as a verified zero.

Research basis, methodology & limitations

These are Wellington AI comparative proxy estimates derived from published industrial cluster and trade context, TNPCB monitoring coverage, existing TN IGS City Indicator planning values, and judgement on the fit of each AI model to the core city and regional catchment. They are not an official rating series and are not statistically calibrated measurements. The 0–100 scales express project applicability, demand, infrastructure readiness, economic relevance and environmental relevance. The baseline is computed as 0.70 × core + 0.30 × region; missing official metrics are not invented. ThamesAI is the river/catchment resilience model; UrbanAI addresses urban drainage. No separate Flood AI.

Rating safeguards

High plantation potential is conditional on environmental permissions, water, land tenure and ecological suitability. HydelAI depends on existing assets and feasible electrical/network integration. Carbon credits and generation efficiency claims require independent verification. City scores do not add to Part IV GVA without measurable incremental production.
