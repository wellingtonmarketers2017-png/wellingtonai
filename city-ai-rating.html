Basis of AI ratings — factors under each project

The table below explains the factors used to assess each AI model. Ratings are illustrative suitability scores, not measured city performance or an AI technology ranking.

AI projectFactors considered for the cityRating evidence required
TradeAIExport demand, local factory capacity, container logistics, trade connectivity, export readinessDistrict exports, industry and logistics records
ThamesAIRiver flood exposure, catchment rainfall, discharge, flood-defence capacity, population at riskRiver gauges, hydrology and flood maps

TransitAIPassenger demand, corridor density, road width, connectivity, investment and emissions savingsOrigin–destination, ROW and transport studies
PlantationAISuitable land, plantation type, CO₂ sequestration, carbon revenue, environmental suitabilityLand-use, agronomy, ecology and permissions
HydelAIHydropower availability, grid connectivity, dispatch efficiency, transmission losses, energy demandAsset register, catchment and grid data
AirlineAIPassenger/business travel demand, airport connectivity, route viability, aircraft utilisation, operating economics, cargo/logistics and emissionsAirport traffic and catchment, route demand, airline economics, cargo and industry-cluster evidence

All models use a common weighted suitability framework: local need/demand 25%, infrastructure readiness 20%, economic value 20%, environmental benefit 20%, implementation feasibility 15%. Technical factors above inform the evidence entered for each dimension. A missing or non-applicable resource should be marked N/A rather than scored as a verified zero.

Existing City Indicator reference

These city characteristics and five indicator baseline/target values are imported from the September 14 TN IGS City Indicator. They are simulator planning data, not independently verified city actuals, and are not substituted for the seven AI actual ratings.

Rating safeguards

High plantation potential is conditional on environmental permissions, water, land tenure and ecological suitability. HydelAI depends on existing assets and feasible electrical/network integration. Carbon credits and generation efficiency claims require independent verification. City scores do not add to Part IV GVA without measurable incremental production.
