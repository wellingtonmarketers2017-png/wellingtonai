RAC recommendation and integration pathway

Keep the existing six revenue projects unchanged: TN Airline, Retail, Weekend Food, Farmstay + Temple Tourism, EOU / Exports, and GST / Fiscal Revenue. Add this State Finance Simulator as a seventh subtask. The simulator consolidates attributable government receipts and TN IGS savings; it must not add gross commercial turnover or GVA to government revenue.

Recommended next phase: connect audited fiscal baselines, project-specific tax capture, actual monthly collections, departmental savings evidence, and debt-service projections. Approval and implementation remain subject to government validation.
